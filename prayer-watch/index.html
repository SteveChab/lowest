The Prayer Watch happens the night before the album comes out, at 10pm Eastern. If that changes, this page and the email will say so. Follow the Bandcamp page to get a reminder.

What it is

A listening party on Bandcamp, the night before each album’s release. Everyone presses play at once and prays on their own while the album plays. No one can see or hear anyone else pray. Type a prayer or request in the chat if you like. No stream to watch, no sermon. If you like the album, you can buy it on Bandcamp.

How to join

  1. 1

    RSVP on Bandcamp.

  2. 2

    Find a quiet place.

  3. 3

    Press play, then pray.

  4. 4

    Have a prayer or a request? Type it in the chat at the Prayer Watch on Bandcamp.

Questions

Do I have to be in a certain denomination?
No. Come as you are.
Do I have to like metal, ambient, or dungeon synth?
No. But if you do, you’ll find the sound familiar.
Why at the same time?
Praying alone together is still together. Knowing others are praying at the same time changes the mood.
