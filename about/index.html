About

Lowest Hallowed is Christian dark ambient and dungeon synth for prayer, with unblack undertones, composed by Steve Chab.

I’m Steve.

I’ve wanted to make long, slow, instrumental music for prayer. It’s also a way to give my music a second life, as these melodies and chord progressions are borrowed from other songs I’ve created.

What it sounds like

Neoclassical ambient with a darker edge. Slow pads, low strings, and keys that shift as a piece unfolds. Each album is meant to be a soundtrack to prayer and intercession.

What “unblack” means here

Unblack is the name for Christian music that borrows black metal’s darkness. It borrows from the cold and the dark and points them at God.

What it isn’t

I’m not an authority on dungeon synth, and much of that scene is game-style fantasy music, but this is not an exact match. If dungeon synth brought you here, I hope it fits well enough for you.

Why “Lowest Hallowed”

It refers to the least holy of humanity seeking God in a place of desperation and fervor.

What you can do

Listen, pray along, and bring someone with you.
